Multi-Agency Planning Document Platform

Click the map

Click any point: an authority area, an RRTS station's influence zone, or the strip along the track. The cards below then show only the documents that govern that spot. Until you click, every loaded document is shown.

Authority areas are drawn as tehsil boundaries from OpenStreetMap, as an approximation: the notified development areas and municipal limits are not loaded. Influence zones are drawn as the Meerut plan defines them (1.5 km at stations, 500 m along the track).

Pick a region, a land use and a rule. The answer shows what each governing body provides, word for word, with document and PDF page. State-wide and NCR-wide documents are included for every region inside them.

Ask for a specific provision in your own words. English questions also reach the Hindi documents through a glossary of planning terms.

Add a document

The app downloads the file, works out how to read it (text, scan, or old Hindi font), suggests its type, year and legal level, and adds it to the index. Check the suggested tags in data/manifest.csv afterwards. You can also drop PDFs into the source_pdfs folder and run python pipeline/auto.py.
